Probate

Probate is charged on a time basis at £250 per hour + VAT (20%). Any conveyancing element within a probate is charged at the conveyancing rate.

The Three Phases of Probate

1

Information Gathering

Working with Executor(s) or intended Administrator(s) to assess the estate — assets, liabilities, and the names and addresses of those entitled.

1 – 3 months
2

Grant Application

Application for a Grant of Probate or Administration, including completing inheritance tax forms. HMRC clearance and Probate Registry processing included.

1 – 6 months
3

Estate Administration

Liquidating assets, paying tax and debts, transferring assets where appropriate, and distributing funds to beneficiaries.

3 – 6+ months

All legal work in our firm is done by one of our three qualified Solicitors: Laurence Mann or Frances Holton. We do not use paralegals or unqualified staff to do the substantive legal work. When you speak to or write to the person handling your case, it is one of us.

An average freehold purchase or sale typically takes about 4 hours of solicitor time, and a leasehold around 5 hours. The overall calendar time depends on many variables in the chain. We have compiled a detailed step-by-step guide to the conveyancing process which is available on our website.

Client Information Notes

Our Terms of Business

These notes set out the basis on which A. L. Hughes & Co. provides legal services to clients. By instructing us you agree to these terms.

Who Will Handle Your Matter

All legal work is carried out by our qualified Solicitors — Laurence Mann and Frances Holton (Partners), and Norman Mann (Consultant, not taking new cases). You will be told who is handling your matter at the outset.

Our Charges

Our hourly rate for Conveyancing and Probate is £250 per hour + VAT at 20%, plus a small fraction of the property value (50p per £1,000 of price, and 25p per £1,000 of any mortgage). We will provide a written estimate at the start of any matter.

Abortive Transactions

If a transaction does not proceed to completion, we cap our fees at a maximum of 50% of the original estimate, or less if less work has been done.

Disbursements

Disbursements (search fees, Land Registry fees, Stamp Duty etc.) are passed on to you at cost. We will not incur any additional disbursement over £30 + VAT without your approval.

Anti-Money Laundering

By law, we are required to verify the identity of all clients. We use an electronic verification service (currently £10 per person including VAT). We may ask for additional documentation in some cases.
